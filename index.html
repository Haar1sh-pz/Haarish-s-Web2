Arduino-Controlled LED Light Show

Intro to Electrical Engineering Lab

Built and tested an Arduino-controlled five-LED breadboard circuit demonstrating digital output control, LED sequencing, current limiting, and PWM brightness control.

01 Connected five LED branches to Arduino digital pins D2 through D6, with an individual current-limiting resistor for each LED.
02 Programmed each LED to turn on and off sequentially with one-second timing, then used PWM on pin 3 to gradually increase and decrease brightness.
03 Verified the design in Tinkercad and on the physical breadboard. Calculated LED currents ranged from approximately 2.23 mA to 3.02 mA, below the 12 mA design target.
