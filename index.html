Arduino-Controlled LED Light Show

Intro to Electrical Engineering Lab

Built and tested an Arduino-controlled five-LED circuit on a breadboard to demonstrate digital output control, LED sequencing, current limiting, and PWM brightness control.

01
Connected five LEDs to Arduino digital pins D2 through D6, with an individual current-limiting resistor in series with each LED branch.
02
Programmed the LEDs to turn on and off sequentially with one-second timing and used PWM on pin 3 to gradually increase and decrease LED brightness.
03
Tested the design in Tinkercad and on the physical breadboard, with calculated LED currents ranging from approximately 2.23 mA to 3.02 mA.
